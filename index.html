Gridline

Built to help buyers see the whole market, not pages of listings. No lead selling, no paid placement, no “deal badges”.

This build runs on a synthetic, deterministic sample of the national market so it works fully offline. Prices, dealers and VINs are generated for demonstration; plug a real inventory feed into js/data.js to go live. Market signals are informational only and are not financial advice. Vehicle makes and model names belong to their respective owners.
